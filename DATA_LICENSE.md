# Data and documentation licensing

No individual-level SINADEF mortality records are distributed in this repository.

Where legally permitted, the authors release original documentation and derived non-identifying aggregate tables under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0).

Original source datasets retain the licenses, attribution requirements, and access conditions established by their providers. Users must obtain those source datasets directly and comply with their terms. In particular, the repository does not relicense SINADEF, INEI, administrative-boundary, digital-elevation, or NASA lightning-climatology source data.
