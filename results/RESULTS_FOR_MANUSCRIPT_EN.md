# Validated results for the manuscript

Study period: 2017-2024.

## Counts
- Nationally validated deaths: **593**.
- Deaths with district and altitude: **591**.
- Deaths above 3,500 m: **510 (86.3% of geocoded deaths)**.

## Altitudinal gradient
- Rate above 3,500 m: **13.02** per million person-years (95% CI 11.91-14.20).
- Rate below 500 m: **0.15** per million person-years.
- Rate ratio above 3,500 m versus the rest of Peru: **35.7**.
- Rate ratio above 3,500 m versus below 500 m: **87.3**.

## Key interpretation
- Climatological flash-density ratio above 3,500 m/rest: **0.51**.
- Mortality-to-flash ratio above 3,500 m/rest: **52.5**.
- Non-atmospheric electrocution high/coast ratio: **1.18**.

The national cohort is used for demographic and temporal analyses; the geocoded cohort is used for altitude, district, mapping, and regression analyses.
