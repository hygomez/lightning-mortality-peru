## Censoring of flash density

`densidad` is zero for **190 of 1,891 districts**. Zero does not mean "no
lightning": the LIS/OTD product does not distinguish absence from below-detection,
and both arrive as zero.

- `LOD_FLASH_DENSITY = 0.412787` (`config/config.R`) is the smallest non-zero value
  the product represents for the climatological period, i.e. the effective
  **detection limit**.
- The **main specification** imputes censored districts at that limit.
- `20_mfr_auditable_lod.csv` publishes **both** readings side by side
  (`dens_raw`/`MFR_raw` against `dens_lod`/`MFR_lod`) plus the percent change, so
  the sensitivity can be audited without re-running anything.
- Effect: negligible across altitude strata (-0.02 % to -0.46 %); material in one
  subgroup only, the Pacific coast (-21.63 %), where 52.7 % of districts are
  censored.

## Stratum labels

The lowest stratum is labelled **`lowland (<500 m)`**, not `0-500`. In v1.0.0 the
old label was read as "the coast", which it is not: the stratum is dominated by the
Amazon lowlands (87 % of its area). Figures are unchanged; only the label. Use
`LOWLAND_LABEL` in code rather than the literal string.

## Key definitions

- `person_years`: sum of annual district populations for 2017-2024.
- `rate_per_million`: deaths per 1,000,000 person-years.
- `area_weighted_density`: total climatological flashes divided by total district area within an altitude stratum.
- `mortality_per_million_flashes`: annualized deaths per 1,000,000 climatological flashes. This is not clinical lethality.
- `posterior_rate`: empirical-Bayes gamma-Poisson district mortality estimate.
- `probability_above_84`: posterior probability that the district rate exceeds 84 deaths per million; this is a literature comparison, not a clinical threshold.

## Repository manifest

`repository_manifest.csv` lists every tracked file with its size and md5, and is
generated by `scripts/90_finalize_metadata.R`. Three files are deliberately absent
from it, because a manifest cannot seal what changes after it is written:
`repository_manifest.csv` itself, and `renv.lock` and `session-info.txt`, which
`scripts/99_freeze_environment.R` writes at the end of the run. The exclusion is
declared in the script rather than left implicit.
